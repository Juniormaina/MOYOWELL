Essential Clinical Notice & Scope of Service

MoyoWell is a screening and navigation guide, not a medical diagnostic service. Scores and generated pathways are intended solely for educational, contemplative, and informational purposes. They do not constitute clinical psychiatric diagnoses, formal medical prescriptions, or therapeutic relationships. If you suspect you may be experiencing a clinical mental health condition, we strongly urge you to consult with a licensed psychiatrist, clinical psychologist, or medical doctor.
