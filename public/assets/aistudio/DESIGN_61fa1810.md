## Brand & Style
The design system articulates an editorial sanctuary: poised, restorative, and grounded in earth-drawn luxury. It deliberately moves away from cold clinical sterilization in favor of organic dignity, tactile natural materials, and haute-couture editorial layouts. 

The aesthetic synthesizes high-end editorial publication principles with mindful wellness architecture:
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Rooted in rich terracotta earth, sundrenched ivory linen, and botanical calm.
- **Editorial Poise:** High-contrast editorial display typography balanced against precise, human-centered body text.
- **Restrained Tactility:** Soft, micro-beveled radius boundaries and tinted, ambient under-glows that suggest physical warmth rather than digital artificiality.
- **Audience & Feeling:** Conceived for discerning individuals navigating modern preventive health, holistic recovery, and personal wellness with clarity, sovereignty, and quiet luxury.

## Colors
The color foundation draws inspiration from raw natural minerals, sun-baked earthenware, and botanical preservation.

- **Primary (`#C77B62` - Terracotta Clay):** Serves as the primary focal anchor, conveying warmth, biological vitality, and grounded reassurance. Used for dominant interactive touchpoints, primary buttons, active state indicators, and editorial emphasis.
- **Secondary (`#183F35` - Deep Forest Botanical):** A foundational grounding anchor. Supplies architectural structure, container contrast, header banners, and high-impact navigational framing.
- **Tertiary (`#D6B66C` - Soft Ochre Accent):** A radiant sunlight accent. Employed sparingly for milestone highlights, badges, verification glyphs, and subtle notification elements.
- **Neutral Foundation (`#292D2A` & Warm Tonal Base):** High-density charcoal handles deep legible text hierarchies without the harsh glare of pure `#000000`. The canvas rests on warm unbleached ivory (`#F7F3EA`), ensuring all interfaces breathe with organic comfort.

## Typography
The typographical hierarchy balances the editorial distinction of `Playfair Display` with the structural clarity of `Manrope`.

- **Headlines & Titles:** Set with deliberate optical rhythm in `Playfair Display`. It commands focus, setting an intentional, unhurried cadence reminiscent of archival journals and luxury print volumes.
- **Functional Prose & Microcopy:** Rendered in `Manrope` to deliver crisp horizontal flow, open letter apertures, and superior optical clarity across small screens, dense health logs, and clinical readings.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Use all-caps with generous character spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to demarcate sections, categories, and metric units cleanly without visual noise.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on a fluid 12-column editorial grid engineered with breathing room, allowing negative space to project calm and order.

- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column layout with 48px canvas margins and 24px column gutters. Maximum readable content width caps at 1280px to preserve comfortable scan lengths.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 32px canvas margins and 20px gutters. Multi-column cards shift smoothly into paired modules.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 20px margins and 16px gutters. Editorial displays step down gracefully using the responsive typography scale to preserve whitespace along screen borders.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Based on an 8px base rhythm (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px). Generous element separation ensures vital health statistics and contemplative text are never crowded.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this system avoids generic, cool gray drop shadows. Instead, it relies on soft tonal layers, warm translucent overlays, and tinted ambient light.

- **Tonal Stratification:** Visual height is established by contrasting warm ivory backgrounds with layered porcelain and warm-clay tinted card planes.
- **Terracotta Ambient Bloom:** Elevated elements utilize multi-layered, highly diffused shadows tinted with subtle terracotta warmth (`rgba(199, 123, 98, 0.08)` to `rgba(24, 63, 53, 0.05)`).
- **Hairline Tactility:** Surfaces are framed with ultra-thin, low-opacity borders (`rgba(41, 45, 42, 0.08)`) simulating the edge of pressed paper stock, maintaining razor-sharp structure on high-density displays without heavy lines.
- **Elevation Tiers:**
  - *Base:* Flat on warm ivory canvas.
  - *Raised (Cards, Modules):* Subtle `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(199, 123, 98, 0.06)`, `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(24, 63, 53, 0.04)`.
  - *Floating (Drawers, Modals, Menus):* Soft diffusion `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(24, 63, 53, 0.12)`, `0 6px 12px -4px rgba(199, 123, 98, 0.06)`.

## Shapes
The shape language uses `roundedness: 1` (Soft), balancing tailored architectural precision with gentle organic tactility.

- **Base Elements (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Form inputs, inline tags, small badges, and buttons embody subtle rounding, offering a disciplined, bespoke look.
- **Container Surfaces (`rounded-lg` / 8px):** Cards, insight containers, and modular panels feature 8px corner radiuses, reminiscent of premium letterpress stationery.
- **Hero & Dialog Surfaces (`rounded-xl` / 12px):** Larger overlays and modal dialogues use 12px rounding, creating comfortable boundaries while resisting overly bubbly or juvenile aesthetics.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled with `#C77B62` (Terracotta Clay) paired with crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`). Set in `Manrope` semibold with `0.25rem` corners and `0.75rem 1.5rem` padding. Subtle hover state darkens toward rich clay with an ambient terracotta glow.
- **Secondary:** Outlined with a 1.5px border in `#183F35` (Deep Forest) and matching text on transparent backdrops. On hover, transitions into a faint botanical wash (`rgba(24, 63, 53, 0.06)`).
- **Tertiary / Text:** Flat, borderless buttons in `#183F35` featuring an underlined hover state in `#C77B62`.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Default State:** Background in warm ivory with an ultra-fine border (`1px solid rgba(41, 45, 42, 0.12)`), text set in `label-md` uppercase.
- **Selected State:** Solid `#183F35` background with soft ivory text, or `#C77B62` with white text for active medical focus areas.

### Cards & Editorial Modules
- **Surface Construction:** Constructed on `#FFFFFF` or pale warm stone, wrapped in a 1px hairline border (`rgba(41, 45, 42, 0.06)`), using `rounded-lg` (8px). 
- **Internal Spacing:** Padded generously with `space-lg` (24px) or `space-xl` (40px) to give health metrics and narrative editorial summaries room to breathe.
- **Header Alignment:** Section heads inside cards feature `Playfair Display` alongside subdued `Manrope` metadata labels.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Fields:** Set with `0.25rem` (4px) soft borders on pale, warm-tinted fills. Text uses `body-md` in `#292D2A`. The focus state highlights with a crisp 1.5px `#C77B62` ring and ambient warm tint.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sculpted square (with 3px soft curve) and circular forms. Checked states fill with `#183F35` carrying `#FFFFFF` check marks; active focus rings illuminate with subtle terracotta accents.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- **Styling:** Border-separated lists using fine horizontal dividers (`1px solid rgba(41, 45, 42, 0.08)`).
- **Timeline Items:** Feature small botanical circular markers `#183F35` connected by warm sand-colored vertical stems, pairing dates in `label-sm` with clinical entries in `title-md`.

### Bespoke Wellness Modules
- **Biometric Metric Tiles:** Prominent numerical figures rendered in high-contrast `Playfair Display`, labeled with all-caps micro-indicators in `label-sm`, backed by gentle terracotta or ochre status pips.
