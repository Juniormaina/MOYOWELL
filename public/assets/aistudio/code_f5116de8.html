Mood & Affect Scale

PHQ-9: Patient Health Questionnaire

schedule 4–6 Minutes
Scale Length 9 Core Items
Score Range 0 – 27 Index
Lookback Period Past 14 Days

Identifies minimal, mild, moderate, moderately severe, and severe depressive symptoms. Built with an automated clinical safeguard protocol actively monitoring for affirmative responses on item 9.

security Active Safety Safeguard: Immediate crisis support routing upon elevated score threshold.
Validated by Kroenke et al. (2001)
02
Anxiety & Somatic Worry

GAD-7: Generalized Anxiety Disorder Scale

schedule 3–5 Minutes
Scale Length 7 Core Items
Score Range 0 – 21 Index
Lookback Period Past 14 Days

Assesses generalized anxiety symptoms, restlessness, nervousness, and uncontrolled apprehension. Evaluates how emotional tension impacts occupational, interpersonal, and daily executive functioning.

Spectrum Calibration:
Minimal (0-4) → Mild (5-9) → Mod (10-14) → Severe (15-21)
Validated by Spitzer et al. (2006)
03
Positive Vitality & Fulfillment

WHO-5: Subjective Wellbeing Index

schedule 2–3 Minutes
Scale Length 5 Positively Phrased Items
Score Range 0 – 100% Index
Lookback Period Past 14 Days

Measures positive psychological vitality, restorative sleep, calm vigor, and genuine everyday interest. Rather than assessing pathology, this index illuminates affirmative holistic resilience.

Optimal Wellbeing Zone: > 50% Vitality Baseline
World Health Organization (1998)
