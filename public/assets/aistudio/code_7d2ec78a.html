3-Minute Grounding Technique

5-4-3-2-1 Method

Acute psychological distress pulls attention into chaotic storms. Take slow deep breaths while engaging each physical sensation step-by-step:

5

Acknowledge 5 things you can see

A spot on the wall, light from a window, fabric weave, tree outside.

4

Acknowledge 4 things you can feel

The texture of your seat, your feet firm on the floor, cool air on skin.

3

Acknowledge 3 things you can hear

Distant birds, passing breeze, ticking watch, your breath rhythmic.

2

Acknowledge 2 things you can smell

Fresh rain, clean cotton, tea leaves, or ambient wood.

1

Acknowledge 1 thing you can taste

Sip a glass of cool water, a drop of mint, or simply your mouth's resting state.

Tap items as you complete them to center your body
